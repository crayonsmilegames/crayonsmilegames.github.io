Frequently asked questions for parents

Is Somi Kids World free?

The base app is free to download on Google Play and includes free basic content. Additional storybook series and other paid content packs are purchased separately through Google Play in the parent area. The full collection of 140 books is not all free.

What activities does the app include?

Somi Kids World includes Korean (Hangul) and English activities, storybook reading, coloring and jigsaw puzzles. The storybook collection has 90 books in VOL.1 and 50 in VOL.2, for a total of 140 books across 11 series.

Are there ads or an app account requirement?

There are no in-app ads, and you do not need to register a Somi Kids World account. Paid content purchases are handled by Google Play, so its account and payment requirements apply separately.

Can we use the app offline?

Content already installed or downloaded on your device can be enjoyed offline. Installing or updating the app, downloading additional content and making Google Play purchases require an internet connection.

Where are family voice recordings stored?

Family narration recorded by a parent is stored on the device being used. Recordings are not uploaded to CrayonSmile servers and can be deleted in parent recording management. Family narration is included with the relevant paid content pack, rather than sold as a separate recording permission.

Which devices can download Somi Kids World?

Somi Kids World is an Android app available on Google Play, with a layout designed for tablets. Check the Google Play listing on your device to see whether installation is supported.
